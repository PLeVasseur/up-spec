[NOTE]
====
This page is a non-normative overview and navigation index for transport-layer deployment prerequisites of the up-rust SEooC requirement family (`+req~ssr-*+` and `+aou~seooc-*+`).
It states no requirement or assumption of its own: every software duty stands in its canonical requirement item (xref:../contract-generations.adoc#req-ssr-requirements-bind[A requirement binds the actors to which it gives a duty]; xref:../contract-generations.adoc#req-ssr-item-reading[How an item is read]), and every integrator duty stands in its canonical assumption of use (xref:../safety/elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc#aou-seooc-assumptions-bind[An assumption of use binds the integrator who claims the scope, and replaces no requirement]).

== Ordinary transport-layer deployment prerequisites

A deployment configuring transports for the up-rust SEooC element relies on validated deployment inputs, target platform capabilities and the transport-layer contracts of each binding, wire and route:

* *Deployment data and configuration validation:* xref:../basics/configurations.adoc[Configuration and deployment data] (including validated construction, resource ranges and single configuration path).
* *Target platform, clocks and atomics:* xref:../basics/platform.adoc[Platform contracts and atomics].
* *Bindings and binding capabilities:* xref:bindings.adoc[uP-L1 transport bindings] (capability declarations, arrival reports, response delivery, shared-state direction, writer integrity levels, deadlines, TTL and middleware waivers).
* *Peers and native ABI agreement:* xref:peers.adoc[uP-L1 peers and fault model] (faulty-peer model, peer admission, staleness and ABI fingerprints).
* *Integrity levels and copy routes:* xref:integrity.adoc[Integrity levels and copy routes] (one level per transport, zero-copy rules, copy routes and upward carrier crossings).
* *Wires and frame metadata:* xref:wires/README.adoc[Wires and the wire contract] (and sibling wire pages xref:wires/native.adoc[native wire], xref:wires/serialized.adoc[serialized wires], xref:wires/xcdrv2.adoc[XCDR2] and xref:wires/e2e.adoc[end-to-end protection]).
* *Middleware-specific bindings:* xref:README.adoc#one-binding[what is specific to one binding] (xref:iceoryx2.adoc[iceoryx2] and xref:iceoryx2-frame.adoc[the iceoryx2 physical frame]; xref:lola.adoc[LoLa] and xref:lola-envelope.adoc[the LoLa physical envelope]).

== Claim-scoped integrator assumptions of use

The assumptions of use that bind an integrator claiming the element's safety-assurance scope for a configuration — covering deployment data, access to transport resources, integrity-level assignment, qualified bindings and wires, and middleware-specific deployment preconditions — live together in xref:../safety/elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc[the element's assumptions of use]:

[cols="3,4",options="header"]
|===
| Integrator assumption of use | Transport-layer scope

| xref:../safety/elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc#aou-seooc-deployment-data[Deployment data is in range, verified and protected]
| Validated, in-range and protected deployment data for transports, routes, capacities, service depths and peer lists
| xref:../safety/elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc#aou-seooc-peer-access[Only authorized processes reach the transport]
| OS or equivalent access restriction to transport resources, two-way shared-state separation, zero-copy writer discipline and message-ID non-authentication
| xref:../safety/elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc#aou-seooc-integrity-levels[Each transport's integrity level is assigned correctly]
| One declared integrity level per transport matching the development level of each deployed process (and xref:../safety/elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc#aou-seooc-capability-ceiling[Data above ASIL B relies on measures outside the element] above ASIL B)
| xref:../safety/elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc#aou-seooc-qualified-bindings-and-wires[Bindings and wires pass their contracts]
| Target-specific, combination-specific contract-suite evidence, qualification-record states, acceptance of limits and deviations (never known failures), qualified `+unsafe+` outside the element and strict mode's qualified list
| xref:../safety/elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc#aou-seooc-iceoryx2-level-separation[On iceoryx2, each integrity level has its own processes, domain and OS user]
| Per-level iceoryx2 domains, processes and OS users
| xref:../safety/elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc#aou-seooc-iceoryx2-limits[A deployment on iceoryx2 accepts that binding's limits, and no known failure as one]
| 64-bit target scope, static publisher data segments, middleware-allocation permit waiver, limits/deviations and known failures on iceoryx2
| xref:../safety/elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc#aou-seooc-lola-assumptions-kept[A deployment on LoLa keeps the assumptions of LoLa that the binding names for the integrator]
| Integrator-kept LoLa assumptions, LoLa configuration protection, assurance over LoLa itself and synchronized process exits
|===

Related assurance governance for qualification records, verification methods and `+unsafe+` inventories lives in xref:../safety/assurance.adoc[Assurance and verification governance] (xref:../safety/assurance.adoc#req-ssr-binding-readme[A binding's qualification record states its limits and its failures]; xref:../safety/assurance.adoc#req-ssr-verification-methods[The verification methods, and what a Verification line asks for]; xref:../safety/assurance.adoc#req-ssr-unsafe-inventory[Every `unsafe` is inventoried and justified]; xref:../safety/assurance.adoc#req-ssr-binding-unsafe-inventory[A binding inventories its own `unsafe`]).
