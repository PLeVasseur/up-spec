= uP-L1 Transport Bindings Catalog
:toc: preamble

NOTE: This page is an informative catalog of shared binding contracts, Classic communication-protocol mappings and up-rust SEooC middleware binding profiles under `up-l1/bindings/`.

== What is specific to one binding

The duties of every binding (xref:interface.adoc[binding interface], xref:lifecycle.adoc[binding lifecycle and waivers], xref:storage.adoc[binding storage and metadata admission] and xref:../route-observation.adoc[route observation, deadlines and TTL]) and the transport-wide assumptions of use (xref:../../safety/elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc[the element's assumptions of use]; overview in xref:../README.adoc#transport-deployment-prerequisites[transport deployment prerequisites]) name no middleware.
What only one binding must meet, and what only a deployment on it provides, is stated with that binding, in items that are specific to it: they name no other binding, and one of them can give the element a duty of its own.

[cols="2,3,5",options="header"]
|===
| Binding | Items of its own | What that means

| iceoryx2 | Requirements (tag `SafetyIceoryx2`) in xref:iceoryx2.adoc[the iceoryx2 page] and xref:iceoryx2.adoc#iceoryx2-sample-layout[the iceoryx2 physical frame] (one of which, on a frame's metadata, gives the element a duty as well), and deployment assumptions of use in xref:../../safety/elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc[the element's assumptions of use] | Its profile, stated for one release of iceoryx2 (xref:iceoryx2.adoc#req-ssr-binding-iceoryx2-profile[The iceoryx2 binding follows one profile, stated for one release of iceoryx2]), its accepted panic path and its named known failures, its static data segments, its service depth, its event IDs, its expired-connection buffer, its arrival reports and its degradation handler are stated in the iceoryx2 page; its separation of integrity levels and deployment limits are stated in xref:../../safety/elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc#aou-seooc-iceoryx2-level-separation[On iceoryx2, each integrity level has its own processes, domain and OS user] and xref:../../safety/elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc#aou-seooc-iceoryx2-limits[A deployment on iceoryx2 accepts that binding's limits, and no known failure as one]. The frame that the binding places in an iceoryx2 sample, what it checks before any of it is used and what it wipes are stated in the iceoryx2 physical frame
| LoLa | Requirements (tag `SafetyLola`) in xref:lola.adoc[the LoLa page] and xref:lola.adoc#lola-sample-layout[the LoLa physical envelope] (one of which, on a frame's metadata, gives the element a duty as well), and, for a deployment on it, xref:../../safety/elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc#aou-seooc-lola-assumptions-kept[A deployment on LoLa keeps the assumptions of LoLa that the binding names for the integrator] | Both kinds of route, its counts and its storage, the boundary to LoLa's C++ code, the life of a subscription and of what LoLa is given, and LoLa's own assumptions of use are stated there. Its panic paths are treated in xref:lola.adoc#req-ssr-binding-lola-foreign-boundary[Nothing unwinds across the LoLa binding's boundary, and a termination path is a panic path]. Its limits, deviations and known failures are those that its qualification record states (xref:../../safety/assurance.adoc#req-ssr-binding-readme[A binding's qualification record states its limits and its failures])
|===
