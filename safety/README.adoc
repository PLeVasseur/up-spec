= Safety and Assurance View
:toc: preamble

[NOTE]
====
These pages provide the safety and assurance view of the specification for the up-rust SEooC requirement family (`+req~ssr-*+` and `+aou~seooc-*+`).
Ordinary functional, resource, transport, communication and Rust-mapping items of the family live in the responsibility-led technical chapters alongside the rest of uProtocol, while assurance governance, fault-model navigation, assurance profiles and element-specific boundaries, build controls, strict mode and assumptions of use live here.

* *Contract families and precedence.*
  How the repository's three requirement families (classic protocol-chapter rules, the up-rust SEooC requirement family and the owned-frame SDK contract) are identified, whom each binds and how a replacement takes effect: xref:../contract-generations.adoc[Which requirements an implementation follows] (xref:../contract-generations.adoc#req-ssr-requirements-bind[A requirement binds the actors to which it gives a duty]; xref:../contract-generations.adoc#req-ssr-contract-transition[What a part declares and claims, and when a replacement takes effect]; xref:../contract-generations.adoc#req-ssr-item-reading[How an item is read]).
* *Non-normative entry map.*
  This page states conventions and navigation only; every normative obligation stands inside its own item block.
====

[[filing]]
=== Filing and scope

Where an item is filed says which responsibility it concerns.
The item's own statement decides whom it binds, wherever it is filed: for a requirement, xref:../contract-generations.adoc#req-ssr-requirements-bind[A requirement binds the actors to which it gives a duty]; for an assumption of use, xref:elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc#aou-seooc-assumptions-bind[An assumption of use binds the integrator who claims the scope, and replaces no requirement].

* *Other implementations.*
  Which implementations the items of the up-rust SEooC requirement family bind, and how another implementation claims one of their contracts: xref:../contract-generations.adoc#req-ssr-contract-transition[What a part declares and claims, and when a replacement takes effect].
* *An assumption of use keeps `seooc` in its ID wherever it is filed.*
  It is that element's assumption (xref:elements/up-rust/assumptions-of-use.adoc#aou-seooc-assumptions-bind[An assumption of use binds the integrator who claims the scope, and replaces no requirement]).
* *Classic protocol-chapter rules.*
  How long a classic protocol-chapter rule stays in force, and what replaces one: xref:../contract-generations.adoc#req-ssr-contract-transition[What a part declares and claims, and when a replacement takes effect].

== The items

An item of the up-rust SEooC requirement family is a software safety requirement, of type `req`, or an assumption of use, of type `aou`: the types that up-spec already uses.
How an item is read: xref:../contract-generations.adoc#req-ssr-item-reading[How an item is read].

=== Identifiers

`+req~ssr-<area>-<name>~<revision>+` and `+aou~seooc-<name>~<revision>+`.
An item's identifier is in its block attribute line and in its `ID` row, which repeats it for readers.

[[markup]]
=== Markup

As in the protocol chapters: a block attribute line that gives the item its `specitem` role and its `oft-sid`, `oft-title`, `oft-needs` and `oft-tags` attributes, then an open block that holds the statement.
The attributes are those of OpenFastTrace (OFT), the tool that traces the items.
After the block come the `ID` row and the ASIL, and the item's ISO 26262 references and its rationale where it has them; a requirement also has its allocation and its verification methods there, and an assumption of use its "Violation" text.

[[source]]
NOTE: A rationale explains the rule as it stands, and an item keeps a source note where a rule or a contract-affecting definition relies on an external source: xref:../CONTRIBUTING.adoc#safety-area[the contributor guidelines for safety items], "Sources and rationales".

How a contributor writes, revises, files and tags an item: xref:../CONTRIBUTING.adoc#safety-area[the contributor guidelines for safety items].

[[tags]]
== Tags

NOTE: A tag is trace metadata: a selector by which a tracing tool chooses a group of items.
It decides neither whom an item binds nor an item's scope, status or conformance (xref:../contract-generations.adoc#req-ssr-requirements-bind[A requirement binds the actors to which it gives a duty]).
Every item of the up-rust SEooC requirement family carries a tag of this legend, and no classic protocol-chapter tag.

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
| Tag | The group of items that it selects

| `SafetyUpRustCore` | the items of the element, with the shared items of the up-rust SEooC requirement family that it relies on
| `SafetyStreamer` | the items of the Streamer, with the shared items of the up-rust SEooC requirement family that it relies on
| `SafetyBinding` | the items of every binding, with the shared items of the up-rust SEooC requirement family that they rely on
| `SafetyBindingPull` | with `SafetyBinding`, the items of pull bindings only
| `SafetyIceoryx2` | the items of the iceoryx2 binding
| `SafetyLola` | the items of the LoLa binding
| `SafetyWire` | the items of every wire, with the shared items of the up-rust SEooC requirement family that they rely on
| `SafetyWireSerialized` | the items of every serialized wire
| `SafetyXcdr2` | the items of each wire of the XCDR2 little-endian appendable profile
| `SafetyE2eWire` | the items of the end-to-end protection wire
| `SafetyManual` | every assumption of use
|===

=== Gate tags

[NOTE]
====
Each repository that covers items of the up-rust SEooC requirement family gets one gate tag, named after it: `SafetyGateUpRust`, `SafetyGateStreamer`, `SafetyGateIceoryx2`, `SafetyGateXcdrV2`, and so on.

* An item gets a repository's gate tag once that repository covers it in OFT's sense, with at least one coverage link of each type the item needs.
* Coverage in OFT's sense says nothing about whether an item is verified (xref:assurance.adoc#req-ssr-verification-methods[The verification methods, and what a Verification line asks for]), adopted or met.
====

=== Coverage needs

[NOTE]
====
The `oft-needs` attribute names the coverage that a tracing tool asks for.

* A requirement met in code needs `impl,utest`: the code that meets it, and the tests that show it, carry coverage tags.
* A requirement shown only by reviewing a document needs `uman`.
* A requirement met in code that also makes a part state something for integrators needs `impl,utest,uman`.
* An assumption of use needs `uman`: the safety manual states it.
====

== ISO 26262

NOTE: Items and text of the up-rust SEooC requirement family cite ISO 26262:2018 by part and clause number only, written `part-clause`, for example `6-7.4.13`, or `part-Table n`; every such number is that of this edition.
No text of the standard appears here, quoted or closely paraphrased, and no table that maps it.

A reference is trace metadata: it names a clause that an item relates to (xref:../contract-generations.adoc#req-ssr-item-reading[How an item is read]).
