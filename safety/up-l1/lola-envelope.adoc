= uP-L1 safety: The LoLa physical envelope
:toc: preamble

The key words "*MUST*", "*MUST NOT*", "*REQUIRED*", "*SHALL*", "*SHALL NOT*", "*SHOULD*", "*SHOULD NOT*", "*RECOMMENDED*", "*MAY*", and "*OPTIONAL*" in this document are to be interpreted as described in https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/bcp14[IETF BCP14 (RFC2119 & RFC8174)]

Conventions and tags: xref:../README.adoc[the safety area's README].
This layer, and whom it binds: xref:README.adoc[safety at uP-L1].
The contract of the LoLa binding, and the words _loan_ and _sample_: xref:lola.adoc[LoLa].

This page states how the LoLa binding places one uProtocol frame in one LoLa sample.
The sample holds an envelope: a fixed header, a routing hint, the selected wire's metadata, padding and the payload, in that order.
The page states the envelope completely, so that an implementation that has only this page can write and read it.

This page holds the six requirements of the envelope, allocated to the LoLa binding (tag `SafetyLola`).

== Whom this page binds

* The six requirements bind the LoLa binding of the up-rust SEooC, as the writer and as the reader of the envelope.
* Every duty of the binding contract (xref:bindings.adoc[allocated to each binding]) and of the LoLa page holds beside them.
* What the metadata holds, and how it is checked, is the selected wire's and the element's; this page states where the metadata stands and what the binding reads from it.

[[req-ssr-binding-lola-envelope-layout]]
== On LoLa, a frame is one envelope in one sample, in a fixed order

[.specitem,oft-sid="req~ssr-binding-lola-envelope-layout~1",oft-title="On LoLa, a frame is one envelope in one sample, in a fixed order",oft-needs="impl,utest",oft-tags="SafetyLola"]
--
*The order.*
A frame *MUST* occupy one sample, from its first byte: the header, the source hint, the sink hint, the metadata, padding, and the payload, with nothing between them but that padding.

*The header.*
The header is 24 bytes.
Each field is an unsigned integer, least significant byte first:

[cols="1,1,4",options="header"]
|===
| Bytes | Width | Field

| 0 to 3 | 4 | The identifier of the envelope: the four bytes `55 4C 4F 4C`
| 4 | 1 | The version of the envelope: `02`
| 5 to 7 | 3 | Reserved: written as `00`
| 8 to 11 | 4 | The length of the metadata
| 12 to 15 | 4 | The length of the payload
| 16 to 19 | 4 | The offset of the payload, from the sample's first byte
| 20 to 21 | 2 | The length of the source hint
| 22 to 23 | 2 | The length of the sink hint
|===

*Where each part stands.*
The source hint starts at byte 24 and the sink hint at its end, and the metadata starts at the end of the sink hint.
The payload starts at the offset that the header gives, and the payload's length is the header's payload length.
A writer *MUST* write each length and the offset as the parts actually stand.

*The version.*
This page states version 2.
A writer *MUST* write `02`, and a sample with another version is no envelope of this page.

*Padding, and what follows the payload.*
The bytes between the metadata's end and the payload's offset are padding.
They exist only so that the payload starts where its alignment needs it (<<req-ssr-binding-lola-envelope-checks,On LoLa, a sample that is not a well-formed envelope is refused before any of it is used>>).
The bytes after the payload's end, to the end of the sample, are not part of the frame.
No reader, check, comparison or other rule relies on what padding or those bytes hold.
--

[horizontal]
ID:: `+req~ssr-binding-lola-envelope-layout~1+`
ASIL:: B
Allocated to:: the LoLa binding
Verification:: test
* *test*
** Envelopes stated from this page before the binding runs, compared byte for byte with what it writes, apart from padding and the bytes after the payload: a frame with a source and a sink, a frame with no sink, a frame with an absent payload, a frame with a present empty payload, and a frame whose payload needs padding.
** The same envelopes read by a reader written from this page alone.
** Two envelopes that differ only in their padding and in the bytes after the payload: read as the same frame.

*Rationale.*
Every part of the envelope is found from the header, so a reader needs nothing but the sample to locate the frame.
One version, written and checked, lets a later layout be told apart instead of being misread.

[[req-ssr-binding-lola-envelope-checks]]
== On LoLa, a sample that is not a well-formed envelope is refused before any of it is used

[.specitem,oft-sid="req~ssr-binding-lola-envelope-checks~1",oft-title="On LoLa, a sample that is not a well-formed envelope is refused before any of it is used",oft-needs="impl,utest",oft-tags="SafetyLola"]
--
*What is refused.*
The binding *MUST* refuse a received sample:

* that is shorter than the header;
* whose first four bytes are not the identifier, or whose version is not `02`;
* whose reserved bytes are not `00`;
* in which a length or the offset, added to where its part starts, overflows or ends past the end of the sample;
* whose metadata length is zero, or greater than the receiving route's declared limit for metadata;
* whose payload length is greater than the receiving route's declared limit for a payload;
* whose source hint or sink hint is not a routing hint of the form that <<req-ssr-binding-lola-envelope-routing-hint,On LoLa, the routing hint is untrusted, and the metadata decides>> states;
* whose payload offset is before the end of the metadata;
* whose metadata says that the frame has no payload while its payload length is not zero;
* whose payload does not start at an address that is a multiple of the receiving route's declared payload alignment, where the binding maps the sample.

*Before any of it is used.*
The binding *MUST* check every length and the offset with arithmetic that cannot overflow, before it forms any range from them.
No part of a refused sample reaches a wire or an application.
A refusal is returned or counted as the element's rules on refused frames say (xref:../seooc-up-rust/errors.adoc#req-ssr-error-no-silent-loss[No silent loss, and each loss counted once]), and it never panics (xref:bindings.adoc#req-ssr-binding-no-panic[No panic on any peer input]).
--

[horizontal]
ID:: `+req~ssr-binding-lola-envelope-checks~1+`
ASIL:: B
Allocated to:: the LoLa binding
Verification:: test, fault-injection
* *test*: one sample for each case above, each refused under its own cause, and a well-formed sample next to each, admitted.
* *fault-injection*: truncated samples, and samples with each field of the header set to its largest value, sent on a route by a faulty peer: each refused and counted, with no panic.

*Rationale.*
A peer writes the whole sample, header included (xref:../README.adoc#req-ssr-peer-fault-model[The requirements hold against a faulty peer]), so every field is input that has to be checked before it is trusted as a bound.

[[req-ssr-binding-lola-envelope-presence]]
== On LoLa, an absent payload and a present empty payload stay apart

[.specitem,oft-sid="req~ssr-binding-lola-envelope-presence~1",oft-title="On LoLa, an absent payload and a present empty payload stay apart",oft-needs="impl,utest",oft-tags="SafetyLola"]
--
*Presence is the metadata's.*
Whether a frame has a payload is what its metadata says: a frame with a payload encoding has a payload, and a frame without one has none.
The payload length gives only the length.

*An absent payload.*
A frame without a payload *MUST* be written with a payload length of zero, and the binding *MUST* report it as a frame with no payload, never as a payload of length zero.

*A present empty payload.*
A frame whose payload is present and empty *MUST* keep its payload encoding and be written with a payload length of zero, and the binding *MUST* report it as a present payload of length zero.

*Bytes with no encoding.*
The binding *MUST* refuse, before it sends, payload bytes for a frame whose metadata has no payload encoding.
--

[horizontal]
ID:: `+req~ssr-binding-lola-envelope-presence~1+`
ASIL:: B
Allocated to:: the LoLa binding
Verification:: test
* *test*: a frame with no payload, a frame with a present empty payload and a frame with a payload, each sent and received, reported as what it is; payload bytes with no encoding, refused before sending.

*Rationale.*
A wire is given only a payload of its own encoding (xref:payload-paths.adoc#req-ssr-wire-encoding-checked[A wire is given only a payload of its own encoding]), so the difference between no payload and an empty one has to survive the envelope.

[[req-ssr-binding-lola-envelope-routing-hint]]
== On LoLa, the routing hint is untrusted, and the metadata decides

[.specitem,oft-sid="req~ssr-binding-lola-envelope-routing-hint~1",oft-title="On LoLa, the routing hint is untrusted, and the metadata decides",oft-needs="impl,utest",oft-tags="SafetyLola"]
--
*What a hint holds.*
The source hint holds the frame's source, and the sink hint the frame's sink, each as the text of its URI as xref:../../basics/uri.adoc[the uProtocol URI] maps it, without the scheme, in UTF-8.
The source hint is never empty.
The sink hint is empty exactly when the frame has no sink.

*What a hint is for.*
The binding *MAY* use a hint only to choose the physical queue or listener that a sample goes to, and to keep a sample that matches no current receive for a later one.

*What a hint is not.*
A hint is no identity.
The binding *MUST NOT* deliver a frame, admit it to a route or count it as a route's own because of its hint.
A frame reaches a receive only once its metadata has been decoded and the receive's source and sink filters have been matched against the metadata (<<req-ssr-binding-lola-envelope-metadata,On LoLa, the metadata is the selected wire's, and is checked before delivery>>).
A hint that disagrees with the metadata changes nothing that the metadata decides.
--

[horizontal]
ID:: `+req~ssr-binding-lola-envelope-routing-hint~1+`
ASIL:: B
Allocated to:: the LoLa binding
Verification:: test, fault-injection
* *test*
** A sample whose hint names the receiving route while its metadata names another source: not delivered to that route.
** A sample whose hint names another route while its metadata matches the receive: delivered by the metadata, or kept for a later receive, and never lost uncounted.
* *fault-injection*: hints that are not URI text, and hints that name a source the route does not declare, sent by a faulty peer: refused or kept as above, with no delivery on the hint alone.

*Rationale.*
Any process that can write a sample can write its hint, so a hint can only narrow where the binding looks for a frame, never decide what the frame is.

[[req-ssr-binding-lola-envelope-metadata]]
== On LoLa, the metadata is the selected wire's, and is checked before delivery

[.specitem,oft-sid="req~ssr-binding-lola-envelope-metadata~1",oft-title="On LoLa, the metadata is the selected wire's, and is checked before delivery",oft-needs="impl,utest",oft-tags="SafetyLola"]
--
*Whose bytes.*
The metadata is the metadata of the frame, in the format of the wire that the route selects.
The envelope gives its place and its length, and the binding *MUST* carry it unchanged.

*What the binding reads.*
From the metadata the binding *MUST* read nothing but whether the frame has a payload (<<req-ssr-binding-lola-envelope-presence,On LoLa, an absent payload and a present empty payload stay apart>>), and only after the checks of <<req-ssr-binding-lola-envelope-checks,On LoLa, a sample that is not a well-formed envelope is refused before any of it is used>>.

*Checked before delivery.*
The binding *MUST* hand a frame to a receive only with its metadata, for the selected wire to decode and for the element to check its identity and its filters.
A frame whose metadata does not decode, or whose identity is not the route's, *MUST NOT* reach a wire's decoding of the payload or an application (xref:native-wire.adoc#req-ssr-native-agreement[A native type is read only under its exactly agreed layout, apart from the encoding identifier]; xref:payload-paths.adoc#req-ssr-wire-encoding-checked[A wire is given only a payload of its own encoding]).
--

[horizontal]
ID:: `+req~ssr-binding-lola-envelope-metadata~1+`
ASIL:: B
Allocated to:: the LoLa binding
Verification:: test, fault-injection
* *test*: metadata carried byte for byte from sender to receiver; a frame whose metadata names another wire, another payload family or, on a native route, another layout: refused before its payload is decoded.
* *fault-injection*: metadata that does not decode, in an envelope that passes every other check, sent by a faulty peer: refused and counted.

*Rationale.*
The metadata says what the frame is, and the selected wire is the one that can read it; the binding only places it, so that no second reading of the metadata stands beside the wire's.

[[req-ssr-binding-lola-envelope-lifetime]]
== On LoLa, a loan exposes only its payload, and a receive view lives within its sample

[.specitem,oft-sid="req~ssr-binding-lola-envelope-lifetime~1",oft-title="On LoLa, a loan exposes only its payload, and a receive view lives within its sample",oft-needs="impl,utest",oft-tags="SafetyLola"]
--
*Transmit.*
The binding *MUST* fix the metadata and the payload's offset when it creates a loan, and write the header, the hints, the metadata and the padding itself.
What it gives a sender to write *MUST* be the payload's extent alone, and that extent *MUST NOT* move or change size while the loan is held.

*Receive.*
A view of a received payload *MUST* cover exactly the payload's extent: never the header, a hint, the metadata, the padding or the bytes after the payload.
A view *MUST NOT* outlive the sample that holds it, and releasing the sample ends every view of it (xref:lola.adoc#req-ssr-binding-lola-sample-lifetime[On LoLa, no subscription ends while one of its samples is held]; xref:bindings.adoc#req-ssr-binding-lent-storage[Lent storage is exclusive, disjoint and stable for as long as it is held]).
--

[horizontal]
ID:: `+req~ssr-binding-lola-envelope-lifetime~1+`
ASIL:: B
Allocated to:: the LoLa binding
Verification:: test, review
* *test*: on transmit, the writable extent of a loan, compared with the payload's offset and length that the header gives; on receive, the view's start and length, compared with the same; a view used after its sample is released, refused where the binding can detect it.
* *review*: at source level, every way that the binding hands out a view or a writable extent, and what ends it.

*Rationale.*
A sender that could write the header or the metadata could change what a reader sees as the frame, and a view that outlived its sample would read storage that LoLa may give to another frame.
